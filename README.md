## ✨ What It Is

Pool Charm is a pool chemistry calculator and reference guide for homeowners and pool pros. Enter your test readings and it gives you a clear, step-by-step dosing plan — with the reasoning behind every step — so you actually understand your water instead of just following numbers.

It works fully offline, installs to your home screen like a real app, and keeps everything on your device.

---

## 🎯 Why It's Good

**Dosing that accounts for the whole picture.**
It doesn't just say "add X." It works through how your chemicals actually interact — CYA changes how much chlorine you really need, acid pulls alkalinity down along with pH, cal hypo adds calcium, dichlor adds stabilizer — and it orders the steps so you don't overshoot or chase your tail.

**It teaches you while you calculate.**
Every recommendation explains what it's doing and why. Pair that with the built-in Spellbook and you can go as deep as you want — it's a calculator and a pool-chemistry guide in one.

**Genuinely easy to use.**
Punch in your readings and get exact amounts, how-to-add steps, safety notes, and when to retest. No account, no setup hoops, nothing to sign up for.

---

## 🧪 What It Does

**The Calculator**
- FC, pH, total alkalinity, CYA, calcium hardness, combined chlorine, salt
- CYA-aware chlorine targeting (not just a fixed 3–5 ppm)
- Algae & slime shock plans (green, pea soup, mustard, black, pink slime, biofilm)
- Ammonia breakpoint chlorination
- Phosphate remover dosing
- Step-by-step instructions with safety warnings and retest timing
- Trichlor / liquid / salt cell / manual sanitizer support

**The Spellbook**
- 9 chapters of plain-English pool chemistry — chemicals, filtration, algae, stains, salt systems, problem water, and more
- Section search across every chapter
- Reads fully offline, so it's there with you poolside

**History & Profile**
- Save your pool profile once — gallons, surface type, sanitizer preference
- Log readings over time and watch FC, pH, and alkalinity trends on a chart
- Back up and restore your data to a file — move it between devices anytime
- Everything stays on your device — nothing is uploaded

## 📲 Install It

Pool Charm is a **Progressive Web App** — it installs straight from your browser, no app store required.

**iPhone / iPad:** Open in Safari → Share ⬆ → Add to Home Screen

**Android:** Open in Chrome → Menu ⋮ → Add to Home Screen

**Desktop:** Click the install icon in Chrome or Edge's address bar

Once installed it launches full-screen like a native app and works with no internet connection.
