Privacy Policy

Pool Charm  ·  Last updated: June 2026

This policy explains how Pool Charm (poolcharm.app) handles your information. The short version: the app doesn't collect personal information, and everything you enter stays on your own device.

Information We Collect

Pool Charm has no account or login and doesn't send your data anywhere. Your pool profile, saved readings, and app preferences are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device. You can export or clear this data at any time from the Profile screen. We have no access to it.

Advertising & Cookies

Pool Charm shows ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. That's handled by Google under its own Privacy & Terms, not by us.

You can manage or turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings and aboutads.info/choices, or disable cookies in your browser settings.

Affiliate Links

The app may include affiliate links to retailers. If you buy something through one, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites you reach through those links.

Children

Pool Charm is meant for adults taking care of their pools and isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes & Contact

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above shows the latest version. Questions are welcome through the Pool Charm GitHub repository.

Pool Charm is an independent app and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Google or any chemical manufacturer.
